Privacy Policy for CyberPath

Effective Date: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CyberPath ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our educational platform located at https://cyberpath.vercel.app.

1. Access and Use of Google User Data

When you choose to sign in with your Google Account, CyberPath requests access to the following information:

  • Email Address: Used as your primary unique identifier to manage your account and course enrollments.
  • Basic Profile Info (Name & Profile Picture): Used to personalize your dashboard and display your name on earned certificates.

2. Data Storage and Security

Your Google user data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) encryption protocols. We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of your information.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CyberPath does not sell, trade, or rent your Google user data to third parties. We only share data when required by law or to protect the integrity of our educational services.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. You may request the deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting our support team.
